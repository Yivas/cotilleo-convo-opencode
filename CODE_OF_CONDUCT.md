# Code of Conduct

Participation should focus on the work, explain disagreements with evidence and respect people's boundaries and privacy. Unwanted contact must stop when requested.

Threats, harassment, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information and repeated disruption are not accepted. People remain responsible for contributions they submit, including generated content.

The rules cover issues, pull requests, any project discussion spaces and public representation of this project, not unrelated communities. Yivas may remove content, restrict participation, close threads or block accounts according to severity and repetition while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of personal details.

GitHub's **Report content** control is available on relevant content. Private or safety-sensitive reports can use [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact); this is a platform channel, not a confidential maintainer inbox. Non-sensitive moderation requests can be raised in [issues](https://github.com/Yivas/cotilleo-convo-opencode/issues). No private evidence should be posted publicly and the vulnerability channel is not for abuse reports. No response deadline or particular outcome is promised.
